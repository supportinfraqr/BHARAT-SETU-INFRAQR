## Users

The product serves the full compliance workflow rather than a single specialist role. Its users include compliance teams, auditors, vendors, workers, and the operational roles represented in the existing application, including store and site managers.

Each role needs access to the parts of the compliance process relevant to its responsibilities and permissions.

## Product Purpose

The product is an enterprise compliance ERP. It brings compliance work and its related operational workflows into one role-aware system so participating teams and external parties can manage their responsibilities, evidence, status, and follow-up work.

Success means that all participating roles can complete their compliance work reliably through a shared system while retaining appropriate access boundaries.

## Positioning

## Operating Context
