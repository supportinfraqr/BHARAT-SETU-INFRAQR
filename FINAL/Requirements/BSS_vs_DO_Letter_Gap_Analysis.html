Requirement‑by‑requirement comparison

D.O. letter requirementBSS statusWhat BSS does today · gap
1. Appointment letters to every employee, including contract labourOSH Code · model format = Annexure‑II Partial BSS auto‑issues an Appointment Letter when a worker–contractor binding is accepted (api/letters.py, bindings.py:_issue_binding_letter), with per‑site appointment‑letter templates and worker self‑service to view/download.
  • Gap: issued only to workers on‑boarded through BSS — not to all employees of the establishment.
  • Gap: the D.O.‑letter model format (Annexure‑II) is not verified/available as a standard template.
2. Timely payment of wages (daily / weekly / fortnightly / monthly timelines)Code on Wages, 2019 Partial BSS has payroll (PayrollConfig.salary_frequency), cost sheets and the statutory Register of Wages (Form XVII) templates.
  • Gap: no selectable wage period (daily / weekly / fortnightly) — everything defaults to monthly.
  • Gap: no enforcement/alerts for the statutory payment deadlines (daily → end of shift; weekly → before weekly holiday; fortnightly → within 2 days; monthly → within 7 days).
3. Wage slips issued on or before paymentCode on Wages · model = Annexure‑III Partial A Wage Slip (Form XIX) template and wage‑slip/−payslip audit specifications exist (register_defaults.py, audit/spec_data.py), plus payroll runs.
  • Gap: no automatic per‑worker wage‑slip generation & issue tied to the payment event; and it isn’t aligned to the Annexure‑III particulars.
4. Welfare facilities for contract labour (drinking water, sanitation, first‑aid, creche, amenities)OSH Code, 2020 Partial Site/employer profiles carry welfare declarations & worker welfare/safety records: first_aid_enabled, canteen_facility, creche_facility, welfare_room, medical_exam_enabled, ppe_provision, safety_training_enabled, etc.
  • Gap: these are self‑declared flags — no evidence capture / verification workflow against the prescribed facility list.
5(a). Grievance redressal for contract labour — resolve preferably within 30 daysOSH (Central) Rules, 2026 Partial A full grievance module exists (WorkerGrievance, GrievanceResponse; worker/contractor/employer grievance pages) with reply → resolve → confirm → reopen flow, plus employer grievance_mechanism and grievance_officer_name.
  • Gap: no 30‑day SLA tracking, reminder or escalation.
5(b). Grievance Redressal Committee (establishments with 20+ workers, equal employer/worker reps)Industrial Relations Code, 2020 Missing BSS records a single grievance_officer_name only.
  • Missing: a formal committee (members, equal representation, disposal within 30 days) is not modelled.
6. Free annual health check‑up for employees who have completed 40 yearsOSH (Central) Rules, 2026 Missing BSS stores a one‑time medical examination/fitness status (WorkerWelfareSafety.medical_examination_status / medical_fitness_status) and a site medical_exam_enabled flag.
  • Missing: no age‑40+ eligibility rule, and no annual recurring check‑up scheduling / reminders.
7. Mandatory electronic registration on the Shram Suvidha PortalOSH Code & Code on Social Security Missing Referenced only as a third‑party portal in the Terms page.
  • Missing: no capture/tracking of establishment registration on Shram Suvidha, no prescribed‑details/document checklist, no validity/expiry reminders.
8. Align existing IT systems with the Labour Codes“technology‑driven compliance ecosystem” Covered BSS is the compliance IT system — CLRA/OSH registers, wage & statutory masters, minimum‑wage linking, digital records and audit specs. (Effectiveness depends on closing the gaps above.)
9. Nominate a Nodal Officer / SPOC with the Office of CLC(C)Single Point of Contact Missing BSS holds site‑level payroll_spoc / contractor_management_spoc and an employer grievance_officer_name.
  • Missing: an organisation‑level Nodal Officer / CLC‑SPOC (name, designation, contact) — to mirror the CLC’s nomination of Shri Sunil Raghunath Mali, Dy. CLC (011‑20893610).
10. Quarterly coordination — video conferences & regional review meetingsengagement mechanism Not software An administrative/engagement activity; BSS does not (and need not) run it. Could optionally track meeting schedules/action points if desired.

What is missing in BSS (highlighted)

Not implemented (must be built)

  • Grievance Redressal Committee (IR Code) — members, equal employer/worker representation, 20+ threshold, 30‑day disposal.
  • Annual health check‑up for 40+ employees — age rule + recurring annual scheduling/reminders.
  • Shram Suvidha Portal registration — establishment registration capture, document checklist, validity/expiry tracking.
  • Nodal Officer / CLC SPOC — organisation‑level single point of contact record.
  • Statutory wage‑payment timelines — daily/weekly/fortnightly wage periods + payment‑deadline enforcement.

Partial (needs extension)

  • Appointment letters — extend beyond BSS‑onboarded workers; offer the D.O.‑letter model format (Annexure‑II).
  • Wage slips — auto‑generate & issue per worker on/before payment, aligned to Annexure‑III.
  • Welfare facilities — capture evidence/verification against the prescribed facility list, not just declarations.
  • Grievance 30‑day SLA — timers, reminders, escalation.
