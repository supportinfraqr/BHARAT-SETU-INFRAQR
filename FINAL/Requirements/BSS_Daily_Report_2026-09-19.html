Headline work — Admin & Letter Templates

Admin — Template Management (Appointment Letter generation)

New BSS Admin → Masters → Letter Templates screen. Admin can create, edit and delete HTML letter templates, defined state-wise (an all-India default plus per-state variants), for both Appointment and Experience / Releasing letters. A token palette inserts {{tokens}} (worker name, BSS ID, designation, DOJ, wage, contractor, employer, site, ref no., date).

Contractor & Employer — Template Generation & Settings

Each site now selects its Appointment and Experience letter templates (Employer → Site detail → "Worker letters"). Every worker of that site then receives letters in that format. Rendering resolves site choice → state template → all-India default and falls back to the built-in layout when unset.

State-wise Experience Letter format

Experience / releasing letters are produced from the same state-wise HTML templates, so each state's format and wording is applied consistently across all workers at a site.

Privacy Policy content

Full Privacy Policy published (Parts I–V: privacy, data-protection & worker rights, international standards, IP, AI governance), plus the Data-Sharing Policy and Terms of Service, cross-linked and reachable from the in-app privacy settings.
