1What BSS Vision AI provides

Face & PPE detection

Detects people and safety equipment at the edge, with inference running on your local GPU or CPU.

Attendance matching

Matches face events to the worker’s BSS identity and produces attendance events for the platform.

Portal integration

Feeds attendance and gate-pass status into the Worker, Contractor and Employer portals.

Local processing

Video and biometric data stay on your network. Only attendance events leave the site.

Outbound edge agent

The agent connects out to BSS with a scoped token. No inbound ports are opened for BSS.

Evidence retention

Recordings, snapshots and evidence clips remain under your control and retention policy.

2Deployment model — your premises

Why the server sits on your site

  • Data residency: video, face images and evidence never leave your network.
  • Bandwidth: camera streams stay on the local network instead of being uploaded continuously.
  • Resilience: attendance capture keeps working during internet outages and syncs events when connectivity returns.
  • Security: no camera, NVR or RTSP port needs to be reachable from the public internet.

3Planning assumptions

Workload

  • Cameras are 1080p (2 MP), H.264 or H.265.
  • Vision AI runs motion-gated detection at 5–10 FPS per camera.
  • Face recognition runs on events, not every frame.
  • One camera stream is decoded once and shared by recording and inference.
  • Storage figures assume continuous recording at the stated bitrate.

Choose a higher specification when

  • Every camera is analysed at full 25–30 FPS.
  • You use 4 MP / 4K cameras or more than two streams per camera.
  • You need multi-site aggregation or long-term video analytics.
  • You have high face-recognition concurrency at entry points.
  • Recording runs on the AI server instead of a separate NVR.

5Realistic overview by site size

4–8 cameras Edge / mini PC

Small site or office. 16–32 GB RAM, optional T4-class GPU, 512 GB–1 TB OS disk, 6–12 TB recording.

16–32 cameras Single-GPU server

Typical construction site. 64–128 GB RAM, RTX 4080/4090 or L4, 25–50 TB recording.

48–64 cameras Dual-GPU server

Large site or multi-block site. 192–256 GB RAM, 2 × L4 or 2 × L40S, 75–100 TB recording.

96–128 cameras Multi-node

Campus or multi-site aggregation. Two nodes, 2 × 192–256 GB RAM, 2–4 GPUs, 150–200 TB recording.

Add a separate NVR for continuous recording, a managed switch for the camera VLAN, an online UPS and a locked rack. Keep a separate NVR as a second copy of the recordings.
