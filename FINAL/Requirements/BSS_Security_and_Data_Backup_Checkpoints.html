1What the BSS platform offers

Worker portal

A portable digital identity with a QR worker card, document wallet, attendance, wages, grievances, welfare and compliance records.

Contractor portal

Worker onboarding and binding, gate passes, attendance, payroll, statutory compliance, document submission and audit-ready records.

Employer portal

Sites, contractors, workforce visibility, medical and health & safety, gate passes, compliance oversight and employer-managed staff accounts.

Built around verified identity

  • One QR identity per worker, verified before a binding is created.
  • Binding-based disclosure: a public QR scan shows only a limited, safe profile. Sensitive identifiers unlock only for a live binding or the worker themselves.
  • One connected record across onboarding, gate pass, attendance, wages, medical, safety and compliance.

2Security at a glance

✓

HTTPS everywhere

TLS 1.2/1.3 on every public host; HTTP is redirected to HTTPS.

✓

Protected sessions

Encrypted, HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite cookies with bounded token lifetimes.

✓

Strong sign-in

MPIN and passwords are stored as one-way hashes. OTPs are single-use and rate-limited.

✓

Role-based access

Worker, Contractor, Employer, Employer Staff and BSS Admin are separated at the server.

✓

Employer-controlled staff

Only the employer owner can create staff, reset MPINs and grant medical, safety, gate-pass or compliance access.

✓

Binding-based privacy

Sensitive data is disclosed only after a live binding. Public scans show a limited profile.

✓

Masked identifiers

Aadhaar, PAN and mobile numbers are masked in lists, exports and support views unless a statutory workflow needs the full value.

✓

Audit trail

Sensitive create, update and delete actions are recorded in an append-only audit log.

✓

Controlled documents

Aadhaar, PAN, DigiLocker and private documents are accessed through authenticated application endpoints with retention controls.

✓

Encrypted CCTV credentials

Camera and NVR passwords are encrypted by the application and never displayed in clear text.

✓

Rate limiting

Login, OTP, document lookup, contact/grievance and public verification endpoints are throttled per identity and IP.

✓

Validated input

Server-side validation, safe rendering, allow-listed uploads and size limits protect every form and file.

4Data protection and privacy

Protected in transit

  • All portal and API traffic uses TLS.
  • Camera traffic stays on the client’s private camera network.
  • No database, cache, NVR or camera stream is exposed directly to the public internet.

Protected at rest

  • Database and media storage are protected at the hosting layer.
  • Backups are encrypted and stored off-site.
  • Aadhaar, PAN and DigiLocker documents are kept outside the public web path and served through authenticated access.

Privacy by design

  • Public QR verification reveals only the public-safe tier.
  • Sensitive identifiers unlock only for a live binding or the worker themselves.
  • Worker consent and binding dates are recorded and auditable.
  • Face images and biometric templates are retained only for the attendance/verification purpose, then purged.

Data you control

  • The employer controls its staff accounts and permissions.
  • Contractors see only workers and sites they are engaged for.
  • Workers can view and correct their own personal data through the authenticated portal.
  • Statutory identifiers are never sent over email or chat.

3-2-1 for BSS platform data

BSS keeps at least three copies of platform data, on two different media or storage systems, with one copy off-site. A backup is only considered valid after a successful restore test.

Your Vision AI / CCTV data

NVR recordings, Vision AI databases, face images and evidence clips live on your infrastructure. They are outside the BSS backup scope — your team owns those backups and restore tests. BSS only receives attendance events from the edge agent.

6Vision AI and CCTV — your data stays with you

Deployment model — client premises

All camera streams, NVR recording, Vision AI inference, face matching and evidence storage run on your own on-premise or edge server. This keeps video and biometric data inside your network, avoids streaming camera feeds to the cloud and keeps attendance working during internet outages. BSS does not host, operate or back up the camera stack; it only receives attendance events from your outbound edge agent.
