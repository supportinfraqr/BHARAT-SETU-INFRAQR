04 AI Audit Methodology

4.1 AI Actions Performed

AI ActionMeaning
CollectGather document / register / challan / return from contractor or HR repository.
ExtractOCR and read key fields (names, numbers, dates, amounts).
ValidateCheck format, validity, signature, seal, date ranges and statutory formatting.
CompareCross-match data across two or more registers / records.
CalculateVerify wages, deductions, PF / ESI contributions, OT, bonus and net pay.
ReconcileMatch totals across Wage Register, ECR, Challan and Bank Transfer Sheet.
CheckPerform a binary rule check (e.g., age >= 18, no minor employed).
FlagMark missing, mismatched, delayed, expired or non-compliant items.
ScoreAssign Complied / Partially Complied / Non-Complied / Document Not Available.
ReportGenerate observation, remark, risk rating and recommended action.

06 Compliance Status & Risk Rating Definitions

6.1 Compliance Status

StatusDefinition
CompliedAll mandatory checks passed and supporting evidence is available.
Partially CompliedMinor gaps — missing acknowledgement, small mismatch, delayed but paid.
Non-CompliedMandatory document missing / invalid, underpayment, under-age worker, PF or ESI not deposited.
Document Not AvailableRequired document, register or return was not produced during audit.

6.2 Risk Levels

RiskTypical Examples
HighChild labour, no labour licence, PF/ESI non-deposit, minimum wage violation, expired agreement or PO, no POSH policy, no Employees' Compensation policy.
MediumMissing signatures, late licence renewal, minor wage/attendance mismatch, missing employee acknowledgements.
LowFormatting issues, holiday list not displayed, minor date typo, optional document missing.
CriticalEmployment of a person below 18 years — immediate stop-work and remediation required.

07 Key Observations & Recommended Actions

7.1 Key Observations

  • Missing or invalid agreement / purchase order.
  • Labour licence expired or deployed manpower exceeding licensed limit.
  • PF / ESI challan not deposited or deposited after due date.
  • Wage Register mismatch with Muster Roll, Payslip or Bank Transfer Sheet.
  • Child labour or under-age worker found at site.
  • POSH policy or Employees' Compensation policy not available.
  • Equal remuneration non-compliance across designations.

7.2 Recommended Action Plan

08 Final AI Audit Rule Logic

Date RuleIf end date < audit date → Expired / High Risk
Manpower RuleIf actual manpower > licence limit → Non-Complied / High Risk
Age RuleIf age < 18 years → Non-Complied / Critical
Wage RuleIf wage < minimum wage → Non-Complied / High Risk
PF / ESI RuleIf challan not deposited → Non-Complied / High Risk
Mismatch RuleIf attendance / wage / name mismatch → Partially Complied / Medium Risk
Signature RuleIf signature or seal missing → Partially Complied / Medium Risk
Document RuleIf mandatory document not available → Document Not Available / High Risk
